Frequently asked questions

Am I too old for braces?

No. Half of our patients are over 25. Gum health matters, not age.

Does it hurt?

For the first 2–3 days after fitting or a new aligner you feel pressure. Then you get used to it.

How many hours a day do I wear aligners?

20–22 hours. Take them out only to eat and brush your teeth.

How do monthly payments work?

You pay a 20% deposit and the rest in equal interest-free payments over 6–24 months.

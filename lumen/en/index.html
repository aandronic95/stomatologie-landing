Frequently asked questions

How much is the first consultation?

The first consultation is free and includes an exam and a priced treatment plan.

Does treatment hurt?

We use computer-controlled anaesthesia and can treat under sedation on request.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, treatments over €500 can be paid in interest-free instalments for up to 12 months.

How long does an implant take?

Placement takes about an hour; the final crown goes on after 3–4 months.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Yes, on Saturdays from 9:00 to 14:00.
